# HyperInkStudio

HyperInkStudio is a monorepo for a tattoo-industry application built from the experience of running my own tattoo business.

The platform brings together the tools needed to manage the tattooing process, including:

Booking — Manage tattoo requests for custom or flash requests.
Flash - Clients can view and purchase flash uploaded by the artist.
Payments — Support credit card purchases and payment apps such as Venmo and Cash App.
Client records — Maintain client histories, including sessions, materials used, notes, and other relevant information.
Artist Page — Artists will have a simple web hub with a few pages, such as flash, checkout, and an about page.
Custom Sites — My own tattoo website is built on top of this system. Potentially, this could lead to artists hiring me to build and design their own custom experiences on top of the system.

## More to come

I am the sole developer. The project began as a loosely structured project to display my work and convert more Instagram conversations into sales. Then I began adding functionality to track day-to-day operations. I recreated it as part of this monorepo, and it is now being developed for use by other artists and as a small business.

I have started establishing conventions and guidelines to provide a more consistent development workflow. These practices are intended to support the project as it evolves and make it easier should I ever onboard other developers.
