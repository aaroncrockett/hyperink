Pricing:

Deposits:

- Are $100.
- If you're a first time client, and paid a $50 consultation desposit, that goes towards this.
- The depostis are not an extra cost! They are applied to the last charge for your tattoo (usually the last two hours of tattooing)

Flash: (designs I have created)

- $100 per session cost.
- $50 an hour during the session (of tattoo time, not counting stencil prep and application, or breaks more than 5 mins or so).
- Flash cost (usually priced between $50 - $150). I use the flash up to 3 times, then it is sold out. If you do not want the design on anyone else, you may pay $50 extra, and it is all yours!

Ready made deseigns: (designs you bring me with no signification changes needed)

- $100 per session cost.
- $50 an hour during the session (of tattoo time, not counting stencil prep and application, or breaks more than 5 mins or so).
- $50 for your design with no alterations (I still have to prep it for a stencil which requires editing)
- If changed need to be made, this is considered a custom tattoo, and will be priced based on that.

Custom designs: (You have an idea, and likely some references, and I execute it for you. No AI references please.)

- $100 per session cost.
- $50 an hour during the session (of tattoo time, not counting stencil prep and application, or breaks more than 5 mins or so).
- A design fee. This varies depending on the complexity. Usually the cost is between $100 - $200.
- Large pieces such as full sleeves and or back pieces will likely be done in chunks.
- The design cost is paid up front with the desposit.
- If you would like additional alterations after the design is presented to you, you may purchase them in two hour chunks of $50.

Black-out tattoos: (as a cover-up or just as is)

- $50 per session cost. (with no stenciling needed)
- $100 an hour during the session (of tattoo time, not counting breaks more than 5 mins or so).
- If a design is needed for edges or negative space, or any reason, there is a charge for that as well. If the cost for a custom tattoo is cheaper, I will give you that price instead.
